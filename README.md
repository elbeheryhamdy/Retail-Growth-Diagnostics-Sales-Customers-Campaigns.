# Retail Growth Diagnostics: Sales, Customers & Campaigns

## Project Idea
An end-to-end automated Business Intelligence and Analytics solution built on the **dunnhumby - The Complete Journey** dataset. The project establishes an automated pipeline starting with data extraction and cleaning via Python, storing and structuring relational data in SQL, feeding a dynamic Power BI system for stakeholder insights, and integrating a Predictive Machine Learning model for advanced forecasting.

## 📊 Key Business Metrics

The Power BI dashboard tracks **20 key business metrics** across sales, customers, and campaigns.

### 🛒 Sales
- Total Revenue
- Total Units Sold
- Total Transactions
- Total Unique Products
- Total Retail Discount

### 👥 Customers
- Total Customers
- AOV (Average Order Value)
- Avg Basket Size
- Purchase Frequency
- Month 5 Active Customers
- Month 23 Active Customers
- Month 5 Total Visits
- Month 23 Total Visits

### 🎯 Campaigns & Promotions
- Total Campaigns Count
- Targeted Households
- Active Campaign Households
- Unique Responses
- Redeem %
- Total Coupon Discount
- Avg Campaign Days
